Camellia
Senemar
A devoted CR7 faithful, and so much more.

Camellia grew up in the Bay Area with Iranian parents who gave her every opportunity — but when they put her in sports, one stood above the rest. From age six, she was a footballer. Not because anyone pushed her, but because she loved it. She went on to play Division I soccer at UC Berkeley, then took that same relentless drive to Fox Sports, to Facebook, Lyft, and eventually into payments at Visa, where sharp instincts and a competitive spirit found a natural home. She has followed Cristiano Ronaldo with the kind of devotion she brings to everything.

Arian
Razzaghi
A Messi man through and through, won't be moved.

At five years old, Arian's grandfather handed him a ball painted with the flags of every nation on Earth, and told him to go see them all. It was 1994 — the year the World Cup came to America. He never stopped running. From the street courts of San José, Costa Rica to Harvard (where he walked onto the soccer team, then chose spreadsheets over cleats), he went on to build companies across Latin America, scale one from Brazil, and somewhere in between logged over 75 countries. He also spent years caring for his mother through illness — quietly, without fanfare — before building his own company from scratch. Leo Messi is the GOAT. This is non-negotiable.

Our Story

The Journey

Met in a stadium in Moscow. Lost each other to life. Found each other again in a stadium in Berlin. Now here, among the vines, for good.

Chapter One · Before They Met
Camellia 1992 – 2018
Berkeley · LA · NYC

A Lifetime With the Ball

A Bay Area kid raised by Iranian parents who believed in giving her every opportunity. At six, she fell in love with football and never let it go. Division I soccer at UC Berkeley. Then Los Angeles — Fox Sports, covering the game from the inside. Then the pivot into tech that took her to New York with Facebook. A player in every arena she enters.

Arian 1990 – 2018
Harvard · SF · Brazil · the world

A Ball Painted with Every Flag

Raised on the street courts of San José, Costa Rica by a grandfather who handed him a ball painted with the flags of every nation and said: go see them all. He walked onto soccer at Harvard, then walked himself off to build. A water company across Latin America. Startups in San Francisco. Scaling a company from Brazil. Facebook. By 2018, over seventy-five countries. Next stop: Russia.

2018
Where They First Met · Moscow

WORLD CUP FINAL
The First Meeting

Luzhniki Stadium, Moscow

France vs. Croatia. The biggest match on earth. Two people who had crossed the world for football, somehow in the same city on the same weekend. Pourmer made the introduction — and Kaveh, Camellia's older brother, was right there too, looking out for his sister the way he always has, knowing Pourmer well enough to vouch. A handshake in the middle of football's grandest stage. Neither of them knew yet what it would become.

Chapter Two · The Years Between
Camellia 2018 – 2024
NYC · SF · Visa

Tech, Coast to Coast

Facebook in New York. Then back west — Lyft, then a pivot into fintech that landed her at Visa, where sharp instincts and a competitive drive found a natural home. The years were full and fast. Moscow stayed in the memory. So did a certain someone she had met there.

Arian 2018 – 2024
Los Angeles · Family · Building

Los Angeles, Building & Caring

Down to Los Angeles. Building his own company from scratch — relentless days, longer nights. And, in the middle of it all, the chapter he doesn't talk about much: stepping away from everything to care for his mother through cancer. Quietly. Without fanfare. Family first, always. Out of those years came a company built right, and a son who showed up.

2024
The Reunion · Berlin
⚽
UEFA EURO FINAL
Where the Attraction Began

Olympiastadion, Berlin

Spain vs. England. Six years after Moscow, drawn back — separately — by the same pull. They met again outside the stadium with face paint still on and the city still celebrating. This time was different. The handshake from Moscow turned into a long conversation. The long conversation turned into another. Somewhere in that Berlin night, the attraction sparked into something neither of them could ignore. This time, neither of them let go.

2024 – 2025
Together

The World, Together

Croatia. Ireland. Italy. Florence. Brazil. Costa Rica. Big Sur. Mexico. Liverpool. Milan. Chelsea. Every match, every trip, every late-night dinner that stretched into the small hours. The story kept writing itself.

September · 2025 · Lake Garda, Italy
The Proposal
She said yes.

He had one idea, months in the making: get everyone there without her knowing. Both families, quietly gathered at Villa Cortine — a 19th-century castle perched above the shimmering blue of Lake Garda. Then, on a boat drifting across the water with the castle behind them and the Italian sun overhead, he asked. She said yes. When they stepped ashore, the people who matter most were waiting. It was the most magical afternoon of their lives.
